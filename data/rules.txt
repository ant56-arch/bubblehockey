# Bubble Hockey League Rules
## General Gameplay
• Objective: Score more goals than your opponent by navigating the puck into their net using the mechanical rods.
• Match Format: Three 60 second periods tracked by a countdown clock. If it is still tied after 3 minutes of game time, match goes to OT. Most goals wins.
• Face-offs: Held at the center spot to start periods, after goals, or when the puck becomes stuck.
• Face-off Rules: The puck must be dispensed from the center slot to start the match. Pre-game, all players from both teams must stay on the center red line. A goal can be scored right off of a face-off.
• Valid Goals: The puck must fully enter the goal chute and register on the scoreboard; bounce-outs do not count.
## Fair Play & Conduct
• No Spinning: Continuous, excessive spinning ("helicoptering") is banned; shots are restricted to one full rotation per attempt.
• No Stalling: Players cannot hold the puck indefinitely. Opponents or refs can start a 5-second countdown, after which the puck must be passed or shot.
• Physical Infractions: Table tilting, lifting, or interfering with an opponent's rods is strictly prohibited and carries penalty/forfeit risks. Only exceptions for tilting and moving the table during play is for a stuck puck in the goal or on the sides of the dome.
## League Specifics
• Format & Setup: Supports singles play on stable, official bubble hockey tables (e.g., Super Chexx).
• Standings Points: Wins award 3 points, overtime wins 2 points, overtime loss is 1 point, and losses award 0 points.
• Forfeits: A forfeit will be issued for the current and future games of a player who breaks the table in any capacity. Exceptions: if the players or anything on the table breaks by wear and tear, this rule is null. Only deliberate actions that bend or break the rules will count towards forfeits
• Authority: The League Commissioner holds final decision-making power on all disputes.
• Tie-breakers: standings are ranked by points, then goal difference, then goals for.
• Playoffs: at the end of each month all players are seeded by that month's standings into a double-elimination bracket.
