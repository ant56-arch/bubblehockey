# Bubble Hockey League: Dominate the Dome!
Welcome to the home of the Bubble Hockey League: a small work league, a bubble hockey table, and bragging rights on the line. Standings, schedules, results and records all live here.
## How it works
• Games: matchups are posted every game day (Monday to Friday). Everyone plays everyone, and home and road sides alternate.
• Standings: 3 points for a win, 2 for an overtime win, 1 for an overtime loss. Standings reset every month.
• Playoffs: at the end of each month everyone is seeded by the standings into a double-elimination bracket, and the winner is that month's champion.
• Records: career stats, playoff history and head-to-head records are on each player's card under Members.
## How to join
Want in? Talk to the commissioner. New players join the schedule on their start date, and their team and record appear on the Members page as soon as they play.
